# Nikki Wallace
## Science Teacher, Director of Research & Innovation

Nikki Wallace is the Head of Research and Innovation and Lead Science Instructor at Crosstown High School in Memphis and a former scientific researcher, bringing 15 years of teaching experience to her work designing interdisciplinary STEM and project based learning experiences. She is passionate about empowering students to tackle real world community challenges through biotechnology, AI, and hands on innovation that expands access and opportunity for all learners.
