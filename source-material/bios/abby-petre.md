# Abby (Csaba) Petre
## Head of Engineering

Abby is a software developer and technical lead, specializing in taking projects from research prototypes to deployed products in startup environments.

Previously, she has led teams as an early employee and as a co-founder at several early-stage startups, specializing in backend architecture and cloud infrastructure for robotics, IoT, and scientific AI applications.

She earned her M.S. in ECE from Georgia Tech and B.S. in EE from UCLA.
