# Generated photography

These six are **not photographs**. They were generated with Higgsfield (Nano Banana
Pro, 2k) against the three real co-design shots in `../schools/`, which were passed
in as image references so the light, the rooms and the clutter carry over. They sit
apart from `../images/` for exactly that reason: nothing in here should ever be
mistaken for something someone photographed, and a future editor reaching for a
"real classroom photo" needs to see the difference before they use one.

`docs/photography-style.md` is the recipe — the ten traits they are matching, the
base style block, the drift list, and what the two batches taught. Do not generate a
replacement without reading it.

All six were generated 21 Aug 2026 in the second batch, after the first taught that
casting has to be named, that the foreground-occlusion instruction has to lead the
subject line, and that the faces-away constraint on the two student frames is a coin
flip unless it is restated there. The two frames with students in them carry that
constraint deliberately: **no student face is turned toward the camera in either**,
which is both the house style and the reason these are safe to publish beside
photographs of real, identifiable adults. Any regenerated student frame has to be
checked for it by eye — the model has failed it once by showing the faces and once by
painting black bars over them.
