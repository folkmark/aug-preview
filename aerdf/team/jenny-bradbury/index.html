Jenny Bradbury

Vice President of Education Innovation and Partnerships

Jenny leads educator recruitment and training for AugmentED’s R&D efforts, builds and manages cross-disciplinary teams, and establishes networks organized around common education goals.

Prior to joining AugmentED, she served as Senior Director of R&D for EdTech and Emerging Technologies at Digital Promise, where she brought together educators, technology developers, and researchers to co-design innovations with and for historically and systematically excluded learners. She helped develop and operationalize the Inclusive Innovation model, an R&D approach rooted in community that elevates the voices of those with greatest proximity to challenges and solutions.

Jenny has also held leadership positions at Discovery Education and the Network for Teaching Entrepreneurship, and began her career as a high school English and Humanities teacher.
